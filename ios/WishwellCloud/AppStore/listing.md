## Description (4,000)

Wishwell is a little room for what you love: the things you're hoping for, big and small, kept together and ready for whoever wants to surprise you.

YOUR WISHLISTS
- Make lists for birthdays, your home, books to read, or anything else.
- Add up to three places to buy each wish, with prices and store links.
- Mark wishes as fulfilled when they come true.
- Everything syncs privately through your own iCloud account, works offline, and stays yours.

YOUR CIRCLE
- Turn on a public account and add the people you love to your circle.
- See their public lists any time you're looking for a gift.
- Every list stays private until you switch it to public, and you can switch it back whenever you like.

"I'M GETTING THIS"
- Found the perfect gift on someone's list? Tap "I'm getting this".
- Everyone else in their circle sees it's taken, so nobody buys it twice.
- The person who made the wish only learns that someone might be getting it, never who, so the surprise is safe.
- If a wish you're getting is removed or already bought, you'll know.

THOUGHTFUL BY DESIGN
- No ads, no tracking, no subscriptions.
- Notifications when someone adds you to their circle or might be getting one of your wishes.
- Light, dark, or match your phone.
- Free. If Wishwell brings you a little joy, you can leave an optional tip.

## Notes for App Review

Wishwell keeps wishlists in the user's private iCloud account. Please sign in to iCloud on the review device; the app works fully without any other account.

To try the circle and "I'm getting this":
1. Settings → Your profile: enter a name and turn on Public account.
2. Circle: search for "Wishwell Review" and add that person.
3. Open their list "Gift ideas" and a wish, then tap "I'm getting this".

Notifications are sent from nujoomdigital.com through Apple's push service. Tips are optional consumable in-app purchases and unlock nothing. Content can be reported from a profile or list, and people can be blocked from their profile's menu.

Before submitting: create the "Wishwell Review" public account with a public "Gift ideas" list in the production environment, using an Apple Account set aside for it.
