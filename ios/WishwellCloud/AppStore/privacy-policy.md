# Wishwell Privacy Policy

Effective: [DATE]

Wishwell is made by Nujoom Digital. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it, and how to remove it.

## Your wishlists

Your wishlists, wishes, prices, store links, notes and fulfilled status are stored on your device. If you use iCloud, they are also stored in your private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. That data belongs to your Apple account. We can't see it, and we don't keep a copy anywhere else.

If you choose the on-device library instead, your wishes stay on that device only.

## Sharing with your circle

Sharing is off until you turn it on. If you set up a public profile, these things are stored in Wishwell's public iCloud database and can be seen by other Wishwell users:

- the public name you choose and, if you include it, your profile photo
- wishlists you publish, including their wishes, prices, store links and fulfilled status
- people you follow, and the likes and comments you post

Each wishlist stays private until you publish it. You can make a published wishlist private again at any time. Other people may already have seen or copied it, and we can't recall that.

We don't publish your email address, phone number or Apple account details. Apple gives Wishwell an anonymous account identifier so your posts can be tied to you; it doesn't reveal who you are.

## Blocking and reporting

When you block someone, their content is hidden on your devices. They aren't told.

When you report a profile, wishlist or comment, the report is visible only to you and to Wishwell's moderation team. We use reports to review content that may break our rules, and we may remove that content.

## Removing your information

To remove your public profile, go to Settings > Remove public profile. This deletes your profile, your published wishlists, and the likes, comments and follows you made. Your private wishlists aren't affected.

Reports you sent are kept for moderation. To delete your private wishlists, delete them in the app, or remove Wishwell's data from iCloud in your device's Settings.

## Tips

Tips are processed by Apple. We receive confirmation that a tip was made, but no payment details. Tips don't unlock anything.

## Importing from the old Wishwell website

If you import your lists from the old website, the email and password you enter are sent only to that website to sign you in for the import. They aren't saved, and the temporary sign-in is ended when the import finishes.

## What we don't do

Wishwell has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. We don't sell or share your information, and we don't use third-party services in the app.

## Children

Wishwell isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and note the change in the app's release notes.
